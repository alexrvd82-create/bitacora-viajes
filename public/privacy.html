Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Travel Maps ("we", "the app") is a personal travel-logging tool. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your email address (via Google sign-in or email/password) and the trips you log: origin/destination cities and countries, dates, transport mode, and calculated distances. We don't collect any data beyond what's needed to run the app.

How we use it

Your trip data is used solely to show you your own statistics, map, badges, and to generate the shareable summary card you create yourself. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) and the app is hosted on Vercel. Both are reputable infrastructure providers with their own security practices.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we only receive your email address and basic profile info needed to create your account — we never receive or request access to your Google account data beyond that.

Your rights

You can delete all of your trip data at any time from within the app (Account settings → Delete my data). If you'd like your account fully removed, email us and we'll delete it within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies

We use only the minimum technical storage needed to keep you logged in and remember your language/theme preference. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact details on our Product Hunt listing or app page.
